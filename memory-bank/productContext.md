# FitZone Gym - Product Context

## Why This Project Exists
FitZone Gym needs a modern, high-energy website that reflects the gym's atmosphere and attracts new members in the Kadikoy district of Istanbul.

## Problems It Solves
- Provides online presence for the gym
- Showcases facilities, trainers, and class schedules
- Enables membership inquiries and contact
- Displays transformation stories for social proof
- Offers clear pricing comparison for memberships

## User Experience Goals
- Energetic and motivating visual experience
- Quick access to class schedules and pricing
- Easy contact and trial signup flow
- Mobile-first responsive design
- Fast, smooth animations (0.3-0.5s)

## Target Audience
- Fitness enthusiasts in Kadikoy, Istanbul
- People looking for personal training
- Group class participants
- Corporate membership inquiries
